RFP 4 · Safety management plan

Six controls that run every operating day.

HSE 01

Zoning & supervision

Swim, paddle and motorised zones separated by buoys; motorised craft use only the access lane at no-wake speed. A lifeguard watches the water at all times.

HSE 02

Weather & sea limits

Checked at opening and hourly. Activities stop in offshore winds, rough sea, lightning or poor visibility, with a set recall signal.

HSE 03

Guest briefing & waiver

Mandatory briefing on zone limits, life jacket use and recall signal. Age, ability and signed waiver checked before issue.

HSE 04

Equipment checks

Pre-use checklist for every item. Damaged equipment is tagged out and removed until repaired.

HSE 05

Risk register

Documented hazards covering collision, drift, capsize, sun exposure and guest conduct, each rated and controlled, reviewed quarterly.

HSE 06

Incident reporting

Ten-step process from immediate assistance through resort notification, written report within 24 hours and corrective action.

RFP 4 · Standard operating procedures

Opening, operating, closing, maintenance and emergency.

Summaries of each SOP. The full signed SOP manual, with checklists for each activity, is attached as Annex E.

SOP-01OpeningBefore first guest
  1. Supervisor arrives 45 minutes before opening and signs in with resort security.
  2. Check weather, wind, sea state and any DMA notices; record in the daily log.
  3. Inspect swim-zone and lane buoys; reset any that have drifted.
  4. Pre-use check on every jet ski, Jet Car, towable, kayak, paddleboard and life jacket; tag out faults.
  5. Set up kiosk, rack and signage; check first-aid kit, oxygen and radios.
  6. Lifeguard takes position; team briefing on conditions and bookings.
  7. Supervisor confirms opening to the resort duty manager.
SOP-02Guest activityEvery session
  1. Confirm booking or walk-in, room number or payment, and signed waiver.
  2. Check age, swimming ability and any medical declaration; for motorised rides, check ID and the DMA minimum age for drivers.
  3. Fit life jacket; deliver the activity briefing with zone map and recall signal.
  4. Launch through the marked lane; lifeguard logs craft out with time.
  5. Monitor throughout; recall at session end or on any weather change.
  6. Log craft back in, rinse equipment, settle charge to room or payment.
SOP-03ClosingAfter last session
  1. Confirm every craft is back and matches the out-log count.
  2. Rinse all equipment with fresh water and dry life jackets.
  3. Post-use inspection; log faults for the maintenance technician.
  4. Secure equipment in the lockable storage; clear and clean the area.
  5. Reconcile sales against bookings and room charges; submit daily report.
  6. Supervisor confirms closure to the resort duty manager.
SOP-04MaintenanceDaily · weekly · monthly
  1. Daily: pre- and post-use checks; fresh-water rinse.
  2. Weekly: technician inspects hulls, seals, fins, tow ropes, fittings and safety gear.
  3. Motorised craft: serviced every 50 engine hours (boats every 100), with engine hours logged at each service.
  4. Monthly: full inventory count and condition grading; replace worn items.
  5. Life jackets: inspected weekly, retired on any damage or fading.
  6. Every action logged per item; log available to the resort at any time.
SOP-05EmergencyRescue · medical · weather
  1. Lifeguard signals recall and launches the rescue board; second staff member keeps watch.
  2. Bring the guest ashore; give first aid, CPR and oxygen as needed.
  3. Call 999 and the resort duty manager and security immediately.
  4. Clear the water of all guests; suspend activities.
  5. Missing guest: count all craft, run shoreline search, alert Coast Guard and DMA.
  6. Written incident report to the resort within 24 hours; review and corrective action.
RFP 4 · Environmental & sustainability

A light footprint on the Sheraton beach and the water around it.

Fuel and spill control

Modern four-stroke craft. Refuelling only in a designated area with drip trays and a spill kit; no fuel stored on the beach beyond the day's need.

Single-use plastic free

Refillable water for guests and staff, no plastic bottles or straws at the kiosk.

Reef-safe sunscreen

Offered at the kiosk and recommended in every briefing.

Monthly beach & sea clean-up

Staff-led clean-up of the beach and paddle zone, open to guests as an activity.

Long-life equipment

Durable kit bought to last, repaired before replaced, and recycled through the supplier at end of life.

Water use

Low-flow rinse station; rinse water directed to resort landscaping where engineering approves.
